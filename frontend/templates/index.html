Metric Selection

Select a data type to view its definition. Stock Equity market trading data containing prices, returns, volume, VWAP and dividend information for listed securities. Bonds Fixed-income securities containing coupon rates, maturity dates, yields and interest-rate sensitivity metrics. Security Master Reference dataset containing security identifiers, issuer information, sector classification and metadata used across financial systems.
Select a metric to view its definition and formula. Daily Return (Closet / Closet-1) - 1 Measures percentage change in closing price compared to the previous trading day. Log Return ln(Closet / Closet-1) Uses natural logarithms to measure returns and is commonly used in quantitative finance. Simple Moving Average (SMA) SMAn = Average of previous n closing prices Smooths short-term fluctuations and highlights longer-term price trends. Exponential Moving Average (EMA) EMAt = α·Closet + (1-α)·EMAt-1 Gives greater weight to recent prices than a simple moving average. Rolling Volatility Standard Deviation(Returns) × √252 Measures price variability and market risk over a rolling time window. VWAP Σ(Price × Volume) / Σ(Volume) Volume Weighted Average Price represents the average traded price weighted by volume. Dividend Yield Dividend / Close Price Measures annual dividend income as a percentage of security price. Yield To Maturity (YTM) Price = Σ(Coupon/(1+y)t) + FaceValue/(1+y)N The annualized return expected if the bond is held until maturity. Duration Duration = (1/Price) × Σ[t × CFt/(1+y)t] Measures the weighted-average time required to receive a bond's cash flows. Modified Duration Modified Duration = Macaulay Duration / (1+y) Measures bond price sensitivity to interest-rate movements. DV01 −Duration × Price / 10000 Estimated bond price change for a 1 basis point movement in yield. Completeness 1 − (Null Count / Total Rows) Percentage of populated values across critical reference-data fields. Identifier Uniqueness Distinct SSM_ID / Total Records Measures uniqueness of security identifiers and detects duplicate records. Currency Distribution Distribution of securities by issuance currency. Average Price by Sector Average(Price) by Security Sector Average security price grouped by sector classification.
